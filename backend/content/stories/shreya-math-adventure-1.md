# Shreya's Pizza Party Math

*Shreya is having a pizza party and needs to divide the pizza fairly among her friends!*

## The Party Plan

Shreya invited 8 friends for a pizza party at her house:
- Adit
- Krisha
- Ravi
- Maya
- Priya
- Dev
- Anaya
- Nisha

That's 8 friends plus Shreya = **9 people total**.

## The Pizza Problem

Mom brought **3 large pizzas**.

Each pizza had **8 slices**.

**Total slices:** 3 × 8 = **24 slices**

**"How do we divide 24 slices equally among 9 people?"** asked Shreya.

**"Let's calculate!"** said Adit, who loves math.

## Dividing the Pizza

Adit drew a picture:

```
Pizza 1: ⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜ (8 slices)
Pizza 2: ⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜ (8 slices)
Pizza 3: ⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜ (8 slices)
Total: 24 slices ÷ 9 people = 2 slices per person (with 6 slices left over)
```

**"So each person gets 2 slices, and we have 6 extra,"** said Shreya.

**"What do we do with the extra slices?"** asked Krisha.

**"We can give them to people who want seconds!"** said Shreya.

## A Fair Solution

Shreya had an idea.

**"Let's everyone take 2 slices first. Then, the 6 extra slices go to the people who want more!"**

Everyone agreed this was fair.

## More Math at the Party

As they ate, Shreya noticed something.

**"Adit ate 3 slices. Krisha ate 2 slices. Ravi ate 2 slices. How many slices have they eaten in total?"**

**"3 + 2 + 2 = 7 slices!"** said Adit quickly.

**"How many slices are left?"** asked Shreya.

**"24 - 7 = 17 slices!"** said Krisha.

## The Juice Problem

Shreya had 6 bottles of juice. Each bottle had enough juice for 2 cups.

**"How many cups of juice do we have?"** she asked.

**"6 bottles × 2 cups = 12 cups of juice!"** said Ravi.

**"Perfect for 9 people and some extras!"** said Shreya.

## Ice Cream Time

For dessert, they had ice cream in 3 flavors:
- **Vanilla:** 12 servings
- **Chocolate:** 12 servings
- **Mango:** 12 servings

**Total:** 12 + 12 + 12 = **36 servings**

Everyone got to pick their favorite flavor, and there was plenty for everyone!

## The Happy Ending

After the party, Shreya thanked Adit for helping with the math.

**"Math helps us be fair and make sure everyone is happy!"** said Shreya.

**"And math makes parties more fun!"** said Adit with a laugh.

All the friends went home happy, full, and tired from playing together.
