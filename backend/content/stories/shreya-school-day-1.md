# Shreya's School Day with Krisha

*Shreya's best friend at school is Krisha. They do everything together!*

## Morning at School

**Ding-dong!** The school bell rang.

Shreya walked into her classroom and saw her friend Krisha sitting by the window.

**"Hi, Krisha!"** said Shreya with a big smile.

**"Hi, Shreya! I saved a seat for you!"** said Krisha, patting the chair next to her.

They sat down together.

## Reading Time

**"Good morning, class!"** said Mrs. Sharma, their teacher.

She opened a big, colorful book with pictures of animals.

**"Today we're reading about animals on the farm,"** said Mrs. Sharma.

Shreya and Krisha listened carefully. They saw pictures of:
- 🐑 Sheep
- 🐄 Cows  
- 🐷 Pigs
- 🐔 Chickens
- 🐴 Horses

**"Which animal is your favorite?"** asked Mrs. Sharma.

**"The horse!"** said Shreya.

**"The sheep!"** said Krisha.

## Lunchtime

At lunch, Shreya and Krisha sat together.

Shreya had a **sandwich** and **apple**.

Krisha had **rice and dal** and **mango**.

**"Want to trade?"** asked Krisha.

**"Sure!"** said Shreya, and they shared their food.

They were always happy when they were together!

## Art Class

In art class, they painted with colors.

**"Let's paint a farm!"** said Shreya.

**"Great idea!"** said Krisha.

They painted:
- Green grass
- Blue sky
- A red barn
- Yellow sun
- Animals (horses, sheep, and cows)

When they finished, Mrs. Sharma said, **"Beautiful work, Shreya and Krisha!"**

They gave each other a high-five!

## Going Home

At the end of the day, Shreya and Krisha walked to the bus stop together.

**"Did you like school today?"** asked Shreya.

**"Yes! Especially art class with you,"** said Krisha with a smile.

**"You're my best friend, Krisha,"** said Shreya.

**"You're my best friend too, Shreya!"** said Krisha.

They waved goodbye at the bus stop, excited for the next day.
