# Shreya and Neel's First Adventure

*Once upon a time, in a cozy home overlooking the mountains, lived two siblings: Shreya (9 years old) and her little brother Neel (2 years old).*

Shreya loved telling stories to Neel, and Neel loved listening, even though he didn't understand all the words.

One sunny afternoon, Shreya had an idea. **"Neel, let's go on an adventure!"** she said with excitement.

Neel clapped his hands. **"Adben-chure!"** he said, trying to repeat the word.

They started in the backyard. Shreya pointed at different things.

**"Look, Neel! That's a flower. Can you say flower?"**

**"Flooower!"** said Neel, giggling.

They found a butterfly. 

**"Butterfly!"** said Shreya.

**"Butter-fly!"** repeated Neel, chasing it.

Then they counted the trees.

**"One, two, three!"** counted Shreya.

**"One! Two! Tree!"** said Neel proudly.

As the sun started to set, Shreya hugged her little brother.

**"Did you have fun on our adventure, Neel?"**

**"Yesyes! Again! Again!"** squealed Neel.

They walked back inside, hand in hand, ready for hot chocolate and cookies—the best way to end an adventure.
