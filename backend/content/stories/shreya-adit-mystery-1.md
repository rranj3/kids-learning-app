# Shreya and Adit Solve a Mystery

*Shreya's best friend Adit loves math and puzzles. Together, they are great detectives!*

## The Case Begins

One day at school, someone stole the **Golden Pencil Trophy** from Mrs. Sharma's desk!

This trophy was for the best writer in the class. Everyone wanted to win it.

**"The trophy is gone!"** cried Mrs. Sharma.

**"We'll find it!"** said Shreya.

**"Yeah, let's solve this mystery!"** said Adit, pushing his glasses up.

## Following the Clues

### Clue #1: Muddy Footprints

They found muddy footprints leading from Mrs. Sharma's desk to the library.

**"Someone walked through the garden before coming to the classroom,"** said Shreya.

**"Good observation!"** said Adit, writing notes.

### Clue #2: A Gold Thread

Near the library door, they found a gold thread caught on the handle.

**"This thread matches the trophy,"** said Shreya, holding it carefully.

**"It was definitely taken through this door,"** said Adit.

### Clue #3: The Notebook

In the library, they found a notebook with the initials **"R.K."**

**"Who has those initials?"** wondered Shreya.

They checked the classroom name list. **"Ravi Khanna!"** said Adit.

Ravi was a quiet boy who sat in the back of the class.

## Asking Questions

They carefully asked Ravi, **"Did you go to the library this morning?"**

Ravi looked nervous. **"Yes... I was looking for a book,"** he said.

**"Did you see the trophy?"** asked Shreya gently.

**"I... I found it on the floor,"** confessed Ravi. **"I picked it up and put it in the library to keep it safe until I could tell Mrs. Sharma."**

**"Why didn't you tell the teacher right away?"** asked Adit.

**"I was scared,"** said Ravi quietly.

## The Hero Returns the Trophy

Shreya and Adit went with Ravi to get the trophy from the library.

They found it hidden safely behind the book section.

Together, they brought it to Mrs. Sharma.

**"Mrs. Sharma, we found who took the trophy,"** said Shreya.

**"But he didn't steal it,"** explained Adit. **"He was actually trying to protect it!"**

Ravi explained everything to Mrs. Sharma.

Mrs. Sharma smiled. **"Thank you, Ravi, for trying to keep it safe. And thank you, Shreya and Adit, for your detective work!"**

## A New Friendship

From that day on, Ravi joined Shreya and Adit.

The three friends became known as **"The Mystery Solvers Club."**

They helped solve problems in school using clues, careful observation, and kind questioning.

**"Being a detective is about asking good questions and listening,"** said Shreya.

**"And using math to analyze clues!"** added Adit.

**"I want to be a detective with you both,"** said Ravi with a smile.

The three friends walked out of school together, ready for their next mystery!
